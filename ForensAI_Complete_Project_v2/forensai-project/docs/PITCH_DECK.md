## SLIDE 5: HOW IT WORKS — 4-AGENT FLOW

**HEADLINE:** The Digital Hive Mind

**BODY:**  
```
Audio Input
    ↓
🎙️ AudioAgent (YAMNet + IsolationForest)
    ↓ (threat detected)
🧠 PredictionAgent (zone risk + spread)
    ↓ (threat confirmed)
⚖️ LegalAgent (SHA256 + IPFS + Polygon)
    ↓ (evidence sealed)
🗺️ GeoAgent (intent + ranger dispatch)
    ↓ (action taken)
Ranger Alert + Evidence Chain + Timeline
```

**SPEAKER NOTE:**  
"When a sound enters the system, the AudioAgent classifies it using YAMNet — a Google pre-trained model that recognizes 500+ sound classes. If it detects a threat like a gunshot or chainsaw, the PredictionAgent immediately checks zone risk and predicts spread. The LegalAgent seals it cryptographically — SHA256 hash, IPFS CID, Polygon transaction. Finally, the GeoAgent figures out what crime is happening and dispatches the nearest ranger. All of this happens in under 2 seconds, and it all works offline."
